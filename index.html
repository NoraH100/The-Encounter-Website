Welcome to The Encounter

The Encounter is the graduates and young professionals convention of the Coptic Orthodox Diocese of the Southern United States. Every February, young adults ages 22–40 from across the diocese gather for a weekend of prayer, liturgy, talks and fellowship with our fathers the bishops and priests.

The 2027 theme and speakers will be announced soon. Stay tuned to our Instagram and this site for updates, and don't miss the Sunday Social. We can't wait to welcome you.

Frequently asked questions

  • What's The Encounter all about?

    A weekend retreat for grads and young professionals built around the liturgy, spiritual talks, small-group conversation and time with our fathers. Each year has its own theme. 2026 was "Do Not Pass By Your Servant" and 2025 was "Into the Deep."

  • Who's invited?

    Graduates and young professionals ages 22–40 in the Coptic Orthodox Diocese of the Southern United States.

  • When does registration open?

    The 2027 date will be posted on Instagram. For 2026, registration opened in mid-September, and both of the last two years filled up. Join the waitlist so you don't miss a spot.

  • Any fun stuff happening on the side?

    Yes. There's a galabeya party during the weekend, game time, and the Sunday Social outing with our fathers after the convention ends.

  • Which airport should I use, and when should I arrive and leave?

    Orlando (MCO) or Melbourne (MLB). Plan to arrive Friday afternoon and leave Sunday evening if you're joining the social. See Travel & Stay.

  • What should I pack?

    Bible and Agpeya, a tonya or veil, a galabeya, your team jersey and more. See the full packing list.

  • What if it's full?

    Join our waitlist. If any spots open up, we'll reach out to you.

  • Where can I ask questions and stay in the loop?

    DM us on Instagram at @theencounter.gyp, and check the waitlist page.

Plan your weekend

When to arrive and leave

  • FridayArrive in the afternoon for check-in and the opening session.
  • SaturdayFull program: liturgy, talks, small groups and evening fellowship.
  • SundayDivine Liturgy and closing. If you're joining the Sunday Social, book an evening flight.
After the convention

SundaySocial

Many of our fathers stay a little longer so we can spend more time together. After the main program wraps up, we head out for an afternoon of games, food and fellowship with the bishops and priests.

  • 2026: Pickleball at Dill Dinkers, Feb 8
  • 2025: The Backyard in Palm Shores, with axe throwing, group games and plenty of food

Details for 2027 are coming soon. If you're booking flights, plan on a later departure Sunday evening.

Limited to Encounter attendees

About the social

  • Who can come?

    The social is open only to registered Encounter attendees, and spots may be limited.

  • How do I sign up?

    Sign-up details are shared with registered attendees. Watch Instagram for the link.

  • What time does it end?

    In 2025 the outing ran from 3:00 to 8:00 PM. Plan a late flight if you're flying out Sunday.
