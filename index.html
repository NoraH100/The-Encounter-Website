Welcome to The Encounter

The Encounter is the graduates and young professionals convention of the Coptic Orthodox Diocese of the Southern United States. Every February, young adults ages 22–40 from across the diocese gather for a weekend of prayer, liturgy, talks and fellowship with our fathers the bishops and priests.

The 2027 theme and speakers will be announced soon. Stay tuned to our Instagram and this site, and don't miss the Sunday Social. We can't wait to welcome you.

Frequently asked questions

  • What's The Encounter all about?

    A weekend retreat for grads and young professionals built around the liturgy, spiritual talks, small-group conversation and time with our fathers. Each year has its own theme. 2026 was "Do Not Pass By Your Servant" and 2025 was "Into the Deep."

  • Who's invited?

    Graduates and young professionals ages 22–40 in the Coptic Orthodox Diocese of the Southern United States.

  • How do I register?

    Head to the registration page. Spots are limited, and both of the last two years filled up, so register early.

  • Any fun stuff happening on the side?

    Yes. There's a galabeya party during the weekend, game time, and the Sunday Social outing with our fathers after the convention ends.

  • Which airport should I use, and when should I arrive and leave?

    Orlando (MCO) or Melbourne (MLB). Plan to arrive Friday afternoon and leave Sunday evening if you're joining the social. See Travel & Stay.

  • What should I pack?

    Bible and Agpeya, a tonya or veil, a galabeya, your team jersey and more. See the full packing checklist.

  • What if it's full?

    Once registration closes, we'll open a waitlist on this site. If any spots open up, we'll reach out to you.

  • Where can I ask questions and stay in the loop?

    Use our contact form, email us, or DM us on Instagram at @theencounter.gyp.
