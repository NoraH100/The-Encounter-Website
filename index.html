Welcome to The Encounter

The Encounter is the graduates and young professionals convention of the Coptic Orthodox Metropolis of the Southern United States. Every February, young adults ages 22–40 from across the diocese gather for a weekend of prayer, liturgy, talks and fellowship with our fathers the bishops and priests.

This year's theme is Walking Behind Him. Join us for sessions with our guest speaker Fr. Anastasi Saint Antony, with the presence of our beloved fathers H.E. Metropolitan Youssef, H.G. Bishop Basil and H.G. Bishop Gregory.

Registration opens November 1 at 8:00 PM EST. Stay tuned to our Instagram and this site, and don't miss the Sunday Social river cruise. We can't wait to welcome you.

Frequently asked questions

  • What's The Encounter all about?

    “The Encounter” is a convention under the auspices of the Coptic Orthodox Metropolis of the Southern United States, dedicated to serving graduates and young professionals. We strive to encourage spiritual growth, fellowship, and professional development. Through this convention, we aim to inspire a generation to genuinely encounter the Lord Christ in their daily lives, deepening their understanding of their faith, as well as their purpose in life.

  • Who's invited?

    Graduates and young professionals ages 22–40.

  • How do I register?

    Registration opens November 1 at 8:00 PM EST on our registration page. Spots are limited, and both of the last two years filled up, so register early. You can add a Sunday Social ticket as part of your registration.

  • Which airport should I use, and when should I arrive and leave?

    Orlando (MCO) or Melbourne (MLB). Check-in starts Friday at 2:00 PM, so plan to arrive Friday afternoon, and leave Sunday evening if you're joining the social. See Travel & Stay.

  • Who is speaking this year?

    Our guest speaker is Fr. Anastasi Saint Antony from the Coptic Orthodox Diocese of New York & New England, with the presence of H.E. Metropolitan Youssef, H.G. Bishop Basil and H.G. Bishop Gregory.

  • Will the convention provide transportation?

    We will share a carpool link so attendees can coordinate rides to and from the convention. Check back here and on our Instagram for the link.

  • What if it's full?

    Once registration closes, we'll open a waitlist on this site. If any spots open up, we'll reach out to you.

  • Where can I ask questions and stay in the loop?

    Use our contact form, email us at theencountergypsus@gmail.com, or DM us on Instagram at @theencountergypsus.

About our guest speaker

Hegumen Fr. Anastasi Saint Antony

Hegumen Fr. Anastasi Saint Antony joined St. Antony Monastery in Newberry Springs, CA in 1997. He was tonsured a monk in 2001 and ordained a priest in 2003, delegated to serve at Saint Mark Coptic Orthodox Church in Honolulu, Hawaii.

Elevated to Hegumen by Pope Shenouda III in 2006, he served as Papal delegate and steward of St. Antony Monastery.

He now serves in the Diocese of New York & New England, focusing on Youth Ministry and Mission & Evangelism, and is currently establishing St. Bishoy's Monastic Order.

About the theme

This year, under the theme “Walking Behind Him,” we meditate on what it truly means to follow Christ.

Beyond simply knowing the way, we as Christians are called to walk in His footsteps, surrender our own will, and entrust our journey to the One who has gone before us.

Through every trial and uncertainty, we are asked to take up our cross and follow Him, discovering that the beauty of the journey lies not in knowing where we are going, but in knowing Whom we follow.

Come. Walk Behind Him. Encounter Him.
